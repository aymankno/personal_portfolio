A Neuromorphic Aircraft Counter for Underserved, Non-Towered Airports

Hello! My name is Ayman Aghel, and I'm a freshman at West High School in Knoxville, Tennessee. This is my first blog post! I want this blog to become a place to discuss things going on in the world that interest me, and to discuss things that I am up to! You can check out the about page on this website, but I love aviation, autonomous systems, and lately: neuromorphic systems and biotech. I am fluent in Python, a coder for my school's First Robotics Team (Java), and I want to learn C++. If something on here interests you, do not hesitate to contact me! For context on the things I talk about here, please check out the rest of this website and my GitHub.

Right now, my biggest project is probably aircraft_counter. It's a manually coded leaky-integrate-fire network currently comprised of 5 neurons: one takeoff, one landing, two touch/go, and one "time" neuron. More details can be found on the repo. It's coded completely manually in Python, so it only uses OpenCV and NumPy libraries. Airports need aircraft-counting technology that can classify movement by takeoff, landing, and touch/go to recieve grants from the F.A.A, and most end up costing small airports tens of thousands of dollars; they can't afford it, so they end up getting super low amounts of money since their numbers are estimates. I thought this was the perfect oppurtunity to improve general aviation safety and learn about neuromorphic technologies! If my counter has within a 3% accuracy of an airport's actual takeoff, landing, and go/around count in testing, it could be used to count aircraft and get underserved, non-towered airports the funding they need. You can see a detailed description of nearly all of the changes to the repository from initial commit on the README.md in the repository. Of course, though, this project still needs to be converted to code capable of being used in neuromorphic chips (and it needs a neuromorphic chip!). Within the next week, I plan to film my own footage at general aviation airports due to a lack of available footage online (static airport webcams). Check out the repository in the projects page of this website!
